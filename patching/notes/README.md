# Patch research notes

One file per patch, written during research and before the patch itself: `COS_<Name>.md`. It
follows the template at `.claude/skills/airkit-patch/assets/note-template.md` — the brief, what
the corpus and references suggested, the gesture-to-sound mapping decided on, and any open
questions for Ciaran. It stays here after the patch is finished: it's the record of why the patch
works the way it does, for whoever comes back to it later (including a future research pass).
